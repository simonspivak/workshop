## Вход и сессии

### AUTH-1 · high · Own per-IP login limiter (100/15 min) counts successful logins and all attempts — a 50-person room can lock itself out for 15 minutes
- **Файлы:** lib/server/rate-limit.ts:17-23, lib/server/rate-limit.ts:30-46, app/(platform)/login/actions.ts:42-46, app/(platform)/login/actions.ts:22, app/(platform)/login/actions.ts:55, supabase/migrations/20261105100000_security_hardening.sql:124-151
- **Доказательство:** actions.ts:43-46 calls takeAttempt(LOGIN_LIMITS.ip) before every sign-in, including the ones that succeed; only the account counter is reset on success (actions.ts:55). rate-limit.ts:21 sets ip = 100 attempts per 15 min (IPv4 address or IPv6 /64, bot/ratelimit.ts:47-53). The whole classroom shares one public IP [research: 'Classroom NAT']. When the counter is exhausted the student sees TOO_MANY = 'Подождите пару минут' (actions.ts:22) although the window is 15 minutes. The counter is DB-backed (rate_limit_hit under advisory lock, migration :136-150) so it is shared across all Vercel instances — the limit really is global for the room.
- **При 10 учениках:** 10 students × (1 success + ~1 typo of a 17-character card password) ≈ 20-30 attempts in 2 minutes → far below 100. No impact.
- **При 50 учениках:** 50 students × 1 success = 50 attempts before any mistake. Each wrong password (13-year-olds typing kiwi-raketa-sova-42 from a card; 30-50 % first-try error rate is realistic) adds one. ~75-100 attempts in the first 2 minutes; a second group in the same room within 15 minutes, or a few students re-logging after a phone lock, crosses 100 → EVERY further login from the room returns 'Слишком много попыток' for up to 15 minutes while the teacher has no unlock button. Lesson start stalls.
- **Как чинить:** (1) Count only failures for the IP rule: keep takeAttempt before sign-in for the account rule, but for the IP rule check-without-increment first and increment only when signInWithPassword fails (add a `p_increment boolean` parameter to rate_limit_hit or call rate_limit_hit only in the error branch; a small race is acceptable here). (2) Raise LOGIN_LIMITS.ip to at least 300 per 15 min (IPv4) — brute-force protection still comes from the 20-per-account rule. (3) Make the message truthful and child-readable: 'Слишком много попыток входа с этого места. Подождите 15 минут или позовите ведущего.' (4) Document in setup.md that one room = one IP and what the limit means.
- **Сложность:** small; уверенность: high

### AUTH-2 · medium · Supabase /auth/v1/token per-IP bucket (30 burst, 1800/h) is shared by server-side sign-ins AND refreshes from Vercel egress IPs; a 429 on refresh after token expiry logs the student out
- **Файлы:** app/(platform)/login/actions.ts:48-53, lib/server/supabase/server.ts:14-34, proxy.ts:38-55, lib/server/dal/auth.ts:57-60, node_modules/@supabase/auth-js/dist/main/GoTrueClient.js:2541-2547, node_modules/@supabase/auth-js/dist/main/GoTrueClient.js:4300-4330, node_modules/@supabase/auth-js/dist/main/lib/fetch.js:32-34, node_modules/@supabase/auth-js/dist/main/lib/constants.js:13
- **Доказательство:** Sign-in runs server-side with the publishable key (actions.ts:49, server.ts:18), so GoTrue keys the bucket on the Vercel function's egress IP, which is dynamic and undocumented in size [research: 'Vercel egress IP']. Refreshes go to the same per-IP bucket [research: 'Auth rate limit']. In auth-js, getClaims → getSession → __loadSession refreshes when the token is within EXPIRY_MARGIN_MS = 90 s of expiry (constants.js:13, GoTrueClient.js:2541-2547). A 429 is NOT in NETWORK_ERROR_CODES (fetch.js:32-34), so it is non-retryable: if the access token is still valid the session is preserved, but if it has already expired _removeSession() runs (GoTrueClient.js ~4310-4318) and the cookies are deleted → proxy.ts:58-61 redirects to /login. Each request builds a fresh client (server.ts:14, proxy.ts:38), so the per-client failure cooldown does not apply: every 5-second poll re-attempts the refresh during the 90 s margin, amplifying any 429 storm. Students who all logged in within the same 2 minutes also all refresh within the same 2 minutes one hour later. actions.ts:52 maps a 429 at sign-in to TOO_MANY with no retry.
- **При 10 учениках:** 10 sign-ins in 2 min plus 10 refreshes an hour later are far inside a 30-burst bucket even if every call exits from one Vercel IP. No impact expected.
- **При 50 учениках:** If 50 sign-ins cluster into ~30 s through one warm instance/egress IP, capacity is 30 + 15 refilled = 45 → the last 5-10 students get 'Слишком много попыток' and must retry blindly. One hour later the refresh wave repeats the same clustering; a 429 there while a student's token has just expired silently logs them out (redirect to /login mid-chat, polls return 401). Probability is medium (depends on undocumented Vercel egress IP pool) but the consequence is visible to several students at once.
- **Как чинить:** (1) In login(): on error.status === 429 wait 1.5-3 s with jitter and retry signInWithPassword once before returning TOO_MANY; change the message to 'Сервер занят. Подождите 10 секунд и нажмите «Войти» ещё раз.' (2) Log { status: error.status, at: Date.now() } with console.error (no e-mail) so the first 50-person workshop produces evidence of how many 429s occur. (3) Teacher procedure in setup.md: log students in in two waves of ≤25 at least 1 minute apart; open the site 5 min early. (4) Do NOT move sign-in to the browser and do NOT enable Sb-Forwarded-For for this flow — the room's single IP would hit the same bucket harder [research: 'Classroom NAT']. (5) Keep JWT expiry at 1 h (lowering it multiplies refreshes against the same bucket). (6) On Pro, test whether PATCH /v1/projects/{ref}/config/auth rate_limit_token_refresh is honoured (unverified [research]).
- **Сложность:** small; уверенность: medium

### AUTH-3 · medium · Per-account lockout (20 wrong passwords / 15 min) lets any classmate lock a peer out, and nobody can unlock
- **Файлы:** lib/server/rate-limit.ts:19, app/(platform)/login/actions.ts:44-46, app/(platform)/teach/actions.ts:300-315, lib/server/accounts.ts:55-64
- **Доказательство:** Account rule: 20 attempts per login per 15 min (rate-limit.ts:19), checked at actions.ts:44 and reset only on a successful login (actions.ts:55). Logins are predictable: <group code>-<first name> (accounts.ts:55-64; the real group uses 770-naomi style, docs/course/770-status.md:27). There is no teacher action that calls rate_limit_reset for an account; resetStudentPassword (teach/actions.ts:300-315) sets a new password but leaves the counter, so after a reset the student can still be blocked for the rest of the window.
- **При 10 учениках:** Teenagers in one room know each other's names; one student typing 20 wrong passwords into a neighbour's login (or a neighbour's phone autofill looping) blocks that student for 15 minutes; teacher's reset does not help. One student idle for a quarter of the lesson.
- **При 50 учениках:** Same mechanism, more targets and more accidental triggers (shared devices, autofill with stale passwords). A handful of students blocked at once with the misleading 'Подождите пару минут' message.
- **Как чинить:** (1) In resetStudentPassword call resetAttempts(LOGIN_LIMITS.account, studentEmail(login)) right after updateUserById, so a reset always unblocks. (2) Add a teacher button 'Снять блокировку входа' that calls resetAttempts for the account (audit it). (3) Consider a soft lock instead of a hard one: after 20 failures add a 30-s delay per attempt rather than a 15-min wall. (4) Tell the student the real wait time in the message.
- **Сложность:** small; уверенность: high

### AUTH-4 · medium · Teacher password reset revokes all of the student's sessions (GoTrue Logout) — student is kicked out within the hour with no explanation, and job polling keeps spinning on 401
- **Файлы:** app/(platform)/teach/actions.ts:307-308, components/platform/GenerateClient.ts:50-59, components/platform/GenerateClient.ts:65-99, app/api/jobs/[id]/route.ts:25, proxy.ts:55-62, lib/platform/messages.ts:40

The student's access token keeps verifying locally (getClaims is offline with asymmetric keys) until exp ≤ 1 h; the next refresh fails non-retryably → cookies removed → page navigations redirect to /login (proxy.ts:58-61). API polls get 401 (jobs route:25) but fetchJob returns null on any !ok (GenerateClient.ts:53) and useLiveJob keeps polling every 5 s forever (GenerateClient.ts:78-93) — the card shows 'в работе' indefinitely. For a chat send the student sees only 'Войдите, чтобы продолжить.' (messages.ts:40) with no link.

- **Как чинить:** (1) In GenerateClient.fetchJob return a typed 'unauthorized' on 401 and let useLiveJob stop polling and show 'Сессия закончилась — войдите заново, задача продолжится' with a link to /login?next=<current path>. (2) Render errorMessages.unauthorized with a login link in GeneratePanel/QuizForm notices. (3) In the teacher UI next to 'Сбросить пароль' add the sentence 'Ученика выкинет из кабинета в течение часа — пусть войдёт заново с новым паролем', and note it in setup.md. (4) Optional: after reset, show the new password immediately and tell the teacher to hand it over before the hour ends.
- **Сложность:** small; уверенность: high

### AUTH-5 · medium · Session cookies are neither httpOnly nor Secure (ssr defaults) although the app has no browser Supabase client and allows 'unsafe-inline' scripts
- **Файлы:** lib/server/supabase/server.ts:18-33, proxy.ts:38-52, node_modules/@supabase/ssr/dist/main/utils/constants.js:4-10, next.config.ts:39
- **Доказательство:** Neither createServerClient call passes cookieOptions (server.ts:18, proxy.ts:38), so @supabase/ssr applies DEFAULT_COOKIE_OPTIONS = { path:'/', sameSite:'lax', httpOnly:false, maxAge: 400 days } (constants.js:4-10) — no Secure flag, JS-readable. No createBrowserClient or client-side supabase-js import exists anywhere in app/, components/ or lib/ outside lib/server (grep 2026-11-08), so nothing in the browser needs to read the cookie. The platform CSP uses script-src 'unsafe-inline' (next.config.ts:39), so any future XSS (e.g. in rendered model output) could exfiltrate the refresh token, which never expires by default [research: 'Refresh token rotation'].
- **При 10 учениках:** No functional impact; latent risk that a single XSS gives an attacker a permanent session of a student (or the teacher/admin, whose session can create students and move stars).
- **При 50 учениках:** Same latent risk, larger blast radius (50 students' long-lived refresh tokens). Not a lesson-breaker, but the owner wants to sell the platform; this is a cheap item that a buyer's security review will flag.
- **Как чинить:** Pass cookieOptions: { httpOnly: true, secure: !isDevelopment(), sameSite: 'lax' } in both createServerClient calls (server.ts and proxy.ts). applyServerStorage spreads cookieOptions over the defaults for every chunk (cookies.js:223-230), so chunked cookies inherit the flags. Verify locally that login, refresh and logout still work (they are all server-side).
- **Сложность:** small; уверенность: high

- Each authenticated request costs one profiles query (dal/auth.ts:62-69, cached per render by React cache) — 50 students polling every 5 s ≈ 10 small indexed queries/s, trivial for Postgres; direct connection limits are irrelevant because the app only uses PostgREST/Auth [research: 'Free vs Pro — compute and connections'].

## База данных и запросы

### DB-01 · medium · Group-page star stats read star_ledger without pagination: silently capped at 1000 rows (PostgREST Max Rows)
- **Файлы:** lib/server/dal/teacher.ts:369-384, app/(platform)/teach/groups/[id]/page.tsx:42,96-110, supabase/config.toml:13
- **Доказательство:** getGroupStarStats selects `delta, reason` from star_ledger with `.in('user_id', students)` and NO .limit/.range (teacher.ts:369-375). PostgREST returns at most Max Rows (1000, supabase/config.toml:13, project default per [research]) and supabase-js does not signal truncation. The result is summed in JS (teacher.ts:380-383) and shown as «Роздано / Потрачено» on the group page (page.tsx:103-108). By contrast group-reports.ts:13-27 pages with PAGE=1000, so the author knew about the cap but this query was missed. Every chat message = 1 ledger row (debit), every image/video = 1-2 rows (debit + possible refund), every bot reply = 1 row.
- **При 10 учениках:** 10 students × ~20 chat + 6 media + welcome ≈ 30 rows/lesson → 300 rows/lesson; the 1000-row cap is crossed in lesson 4: from then on «Потрачено» and «Роздано» on the group page under-count silently (the teacher sees budget numbers that stop growing).
- **При 50 учениках:** 50 students × 30 rows ≈ 1500 rows after the FIRST 75-minute workshop → the group page is already wrong at the end of lesson 1; a teacher deciding whether to grant more stars sees spending that is 30-60% too low. With 5 lessons ≈ 7500 rows, the card shows roughly 1/7 of real spending.
- **Как чинить:** Aggregate in SQL instead of fetching rows: add a security-definer RPC `group_star_stats(p_group uuid) returns table(given bigint, spent bigint)` (grant execute to service_role only) that does `select sum(case when reason in ('debit','refund','bot_reply') then -delta else 0 end), sum(case when reason not in (...) then delta else 0 end) from star_ledger l join profiles p on p.id=l.user_id where p.group_id=p_group`, and call it from getGroupStarStats.
